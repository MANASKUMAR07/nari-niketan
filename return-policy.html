Return Eligibility

Your return will be accepted if ALL of the following conditions are met:

  • Return request is raised within 7 days of the delivery date
  • Item is unused and unwashed — no signs of wear, stains, or alterations
  • All original tags, labels, and packaging are intact and attached
  • Item is accompanied by the original invoice / order confirmation
  • The item is not listed under Non-Returnable categories below
  • Item was not purchased during a flash sale or clearance event

Sarees

Returnable if unused & tags intact. Blouse piece must be unpinned.

Suits / Dress Material

Returnable if unstitched and in original fold with all pieces.

‘—

Lehengas

Returnable if all pieces (lehenga, choli, dupatta) are returned together.

‘

Kurtas / Tops

Returnable if unworn, unwashed, with original tags.

Dupattas / Stoles

Returnable if folded in original shape with tags intact.

Western Wear

Returnable if unworn, with all tags and original packaging.

Non-Returnable Items

⚠ The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances
  • Innerwear, lingerie, and undergarments — For hygiene and safety reasons (as per Indian Consumer Hygiene Guidelines)
  • Stitched / Tailored / Altered garments — Custom stitching voids the return eligibility
  • Items used in ceremonies, functions, or events — Worn items are not accepted
  • Washed, dry-cleaned, or ironed items — Post-care items are non-returnable
  • Items with removed or tampered tags
  • Items purchased during clearance / final sale events
  • Digital goods, gift cards, or store credit vouchers
  • Items damaged due to customer mishandling
  • Blouse pieces that have been cut or stitched
Important Notice on Hygiene Items

As per the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and common Indian retail practice, innerwear and intimate apparel items are strictly non-returnable once the original packaging is opened. This policy exists to protect public health and safety.

How to Initiate a Return

1

Go to My Orders

Log into your account and navigate to My Orders. Find the delivered order you wish to return.

2

Click "↺ Return Order"

Click the Return button (only visible for delivered orders within the 7-day window). Fill in the return reason and provide photos if the item is defective.

3

Return Request Review (24–48 hrs)

Our team reviews your return request. You'll receive a confirmation via email/WhatsApp with the pickup details.

4

Pickup Arranged

We arrange a reverse pickup from your delivery address. Keep the item securely packed in its original packaging. Our courier partner will collect it within 2–3 business days.

5

Quality Check & Refund

Once the item is received and passes our quality inspection, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days.

Exchange Policy

We offer size/color exchanges for eligible items within 7 days of delivery.

  • Exchange is available for size mismatch or color variation from what was ordered
  • The replacement item must be of equal or lesser value (no additional charge for equal value)
  • Subject to stock availability — if the desired size/color is unavailable, a full refund will be issued
  • Exchange items follow the same eligibility conditions as returns (unused, tags intact, etc.)
  • Only one exchange is permitted per order
⚠

Defective, Damaged or Wrong Items

Your Rights Under Consumer Protection Act 2019

If you receive a defective, damaged, or wrong item, you are entitled to:

  • A full refund including any delivery charges paid
  • A free replacement of the correct/undamaged item
  • These rights apply regardless of the 7-day window if the damage/defect was present at delivery

What to do if you receive a damaged or wrong item:

  1. Take clear photographs of the item, packaging, and shipping label before use
  2. Initiate a return from My Orders within 48 hours of delivery and select "Defective / Damaged" as the reason
  3. Upload the photos as evidence in the return form
  4. We will arrange an immediate free reverse pickup at no cost to you

Your Legal Rights (India)

This policy is framed in accordance with the following Indian laws and regulations:

Consumer Protection Act, 2019 ’ Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 📄 Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)
  • Under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, you have the right to seek redressal for defective goods, unfair trade practices, and restrictive trade practices.
  • You may file a complaint with the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) or State Consumer Forum if your grievance is not resolved within 30 days.
  • Online complaints can be filed at consumerhelpline.gov.in or call 1800-11-4000 (toll-free).
  • The E-Commerce Rules 2020 mandate that sellers display a clear return, refund, and exchange policy — which this page fulfills.
⚠ Dispute Resolution

In case of any dispute, Nari Niketan will make every effort to resolve the matter amicably. If unresolved within 30 days, you may approach the appropriate Consumer Forum in Sonbhadra district, Uttar Pradesh, which has jurisdiction over our business.
