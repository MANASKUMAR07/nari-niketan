4. Government Consumer Dispute Channels & Jurisdiction

External Government Redressal Avenues

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided by our Grievance Officer, you may approach statutory authorities:

  • National Consumer Helpline (NCH): Dial toll-free number 1915 or 1800-11-4000 (Govt. of India), or register online at consumerhelpline.gov.in →.
  • e-Daakhil Online Consumer Filing: Consumers can file consumer cases electronically via the Government of India portal edaakhil.nic.in →.
  • District Consumer Commission: Jurisdiction lies with the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (DCDRC) in Sonbhadra, Uttar Pradesh.
